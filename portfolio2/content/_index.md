I am a Senior Research Fellow at [Massachusetts General Hospital](https://www.massgeneral.org) and [Harvard Medical School](https://hms.harvard.edu), working at the intersection of **immunology**, **neuroscience**, and **quantitative biology**.

My research uses single-cell genomics and high-resolution microscopy to understand how early-life adversity — particularly prenatal stress — shapes immune function and brain development over a lifetime.

Before this, I completed my PhD in Physics at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR)](https://www.tifr.res.in), Mumbai, where I built computational tools to study cell cycle-dependent DNA damage responses and genome architecture from fluorescence microscopy images.
